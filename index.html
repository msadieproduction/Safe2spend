How Safe2Spend uses my numbers ›

Think of Safe2Spend like a financial calculator. The amounts you enter are used to calculate your plan and are saved in this browser so your plan is still here when you return. Safe2Spend does not log in to your bank, read transactions, or independently monitor your spending.
